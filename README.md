# AI Evaluation Samples

A portfolio of original AI-response evaluations focused on identifying model failures, explaining why they matter, and proposing stronger alternatives.

## What I Evaluate

My evaluations focus on areas including:

- Factual accuracy
- Instruction following
- Preservation of uncertainty
- Hallucination and unsupported inference
- Attribution
- Ambiguity handling
- Editorial quality
- Constraint adherence
- Safety and potential real-world harm

Rather than evaluating responses solely for surface-level correctness, I look for failures in reasoning, interpretation, and evidence handling that may make an otherwise plausible response unreliable.

## Evaluation Approach

Each case study begins with a controlled prompt and model response created specifically for evaluation.

I then:

1. Identify explicit and implicit requirements.
2. Separate factual errors from stylistic preferences.
3. Trace claims back to the available source information.
4. Identify unsupported assumptions or changes in certainty.
5. Consider the potential consequences of the model's errors.
6. Recommend a more accurate approach.
